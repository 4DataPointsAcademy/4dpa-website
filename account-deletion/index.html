Account & Privacy

Delete Your Account

This page explains how to delete your account in the 4 Data Points Academy app, published by 4 Data Points Academy, Inc., and what happens to your data when you do.

Option 1: Delete your account in the app (fastest)

  1. Open the 4 Data Points Academy app and sign in.
  2. Tap Account & Privacy.
  3. Scroll to Delete My Account.
  4. Type DELETE in the confirmation box and tap Delete My Account.
  5. Confirm. Your account is deleted right away and you are signed out.

Option 2: Request deletion here

If you no longer have the app or can't sign in, submit this form. Use the email address on your account. We'll email you a link to confirm the request is yours. Once you click it and confirm, your account is deleted right away. If anything prevents that, we complete the request within 30 days.

Request account deletion

You can also email info@4datapoints.com with the subject line 4DPA Account Deletion Request, from the email address on your account.

What we delete

  • Your app login and account profile
  • Your program access, course and lesson progress, quiz and workbook answers, and saved tool work
  • Your communication preferences, notifications and device registrations
  • Your event and retreat intake answers, RSVPs and directory profile
  • Other app data tied to your account

What we keep, and for how long

  • Purchase and payment records: kept for up to 7 years for tax and accounting purposes.
  • Signed agreements and legal documents you submitted for a service, such as land trust paperwork: kept as long as the law or the service requires, no longer linked to an app account.
  • A record that your deletion request was made and completed: kept to show we honored it.

Deleting your app account does not cancel a separate program enrollment or payment plan. Contact us if you also want to cancel those. Learn more in our Privacy Policy.

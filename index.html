Real estate mentorship · Why people come here last

About a third of our students came from other programs.

Most of them tell the same story. They paid for classes, a login and a few months of calls, then got left on their own right when a real deal showed up. We built 4DPA to be the opposite.

We have one rule. We are the last stop. If a student feels they need to go somewhere else for something, we bring that thing into our program for them. If anyone has to go outside 4DPA to get what they need, that's on us.

Your mentor

Odis D. James III

Broker, investor, entrepreneur, development consultant and author. Odis oversaw about 2,700 apartment units and roughly $650 million in assets in the multifamily commercial industry before founding 4 Data Points Academy. He built a mortgage brokerage that funded close to $1 billion in loans and a national notary signing company.

He has written three books on entrepreneurship and four on real estate. On every deal he teaches four questions: What does it cost? What does it cost to fix up? What will it be worth when it's done? How much income can it generate?

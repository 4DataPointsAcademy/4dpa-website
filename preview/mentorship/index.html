Meet your mentor

Odis D. James III

Investor, entrepreneur, author and founder of 4 Data Points Academy and VentureU.

Odis grew up going to real estate investment meetings with his father, who has been licensed since 1971. After serving in the U.S. Navy, he started as a leasing consultant in 2001 and rose to the corporate level in multifamily, overseeing 2,860 apartment units and more than $650 million in assets. As a mortgage broker he funded more than $1 billion in loans.

Today he is a licensed real estate broker in California and Georgia, a full-time investor and the author of seven books. He built 4 Data Points Academy to teach investors the same standard he uses on his own deals.

Questions people ask

Before you enroll

Do I need experience or a lot of money?

No. You don't need prior experience or a big savings account. You need guidance and a plan, and that's what we build with you.

How much time does it take?

Live classes are three evenings a week, Tuesday to Thursday at 5:00 PM Pacific, and every class is recorded. Most students fit it around a full-time job and study the library on their own schedule.

Do I have to apply?

No. Choose the program that fits and enroll. Onboarding starts right away. If you're unsure which level is right, book a free mentorship call first.

What does "no end date" mean?

Once your tuition is paid in full, your access and support continue for as long as you're building. There are no monthly fees to keep your access. Access is subject to our Terms of Service.

What's the difference between the levels?

Group Curriculum gives you the full curriculum, live classes and the student community. One-on-One adds hands-on help on your own deals and priority access to Odis. Jumpstart Business Premier adds a done-for-you business setup and a year of business consulting.

Is there a monthly fee?

Not for tuition. The only recurring charge is the optional Empire Cornerstone service in Jumpstart Business Premier: $97/month, billed separately once your business build is live.

What if I miss a live class?

Classes are recorded and added to the library, so you can catch up on your own time.

Do you offer refunds?

All sales are final, so please ask any questions before you enroll. Book a free mentorship call or call 702-710-8030.

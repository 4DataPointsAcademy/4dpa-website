The method behind the name

Every deal begins with four data points.

Four numbers. One informed investment decision. Every student learns to answer these before spending a dollar.

Illustration: a two-family flat from purchase to finished rental
  1. 01

    Cost to buy

    The purchase price is only the first number. Learn to see past boarded windows to the real cost of acquiring the property.

  2. 02

    Cost to rehab

    Roof, windows, siding, porch, systems. Budget every line item before you buy, not after the scaffolding goes up.

  3. 03

    After-repair value

    What comparable renovated properties actually sell for, not what you hope the property is worth.

  4. 04

    Income potential

    Realistic rents, vacancy and expenses, so you know whether the income carries the property and its debt.

Meet your mentor

Odis D. James III

Investor, entrepreneur, author and founder of 4 Data Points Academy and VentureU.

Odis grew up going to real estate investment meetings with his father, who has been licensed since 1971. After serving in the U.S. Navy, he started as a leasing consultant in 2001 and rose to the corporate level in multifamily, overseeing 2,860 apartment units and more than $650 million in assets. As a mortgage broker he funded more than $1 billion in loans.

Today he is a licensed real estate broker in California and Georgia, a full-time investor and the author of seven books. He built 4 Data Points Academy to teach investors the same standard he uses on his own deals.

No. Choose the program that fits and enroll. Onboarding starts right away. If you're unsure which level is right, speak with our team first.

There is no end date. You pay once and keep your access and support for as long as you're building, subject to program terms.

Classes are recorded and added to the library, so you can catch up on your own time.

Is this only for residential?

No. The curriculum covers residential and multifamily commercial, plus creative financing, land and asset protection.

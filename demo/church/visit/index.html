Questions first-time guests ask

What should I wear?

Whatever you're comfortable in. You'll see jeans and you'll see suits.

Will I be singled out?

Never. No standing up, no name tags unless you want one.

Is there something for my teenager?

Students sit with their families on Sunday and have their own night on Wednesdays.

Do you offer Spanish?

Yes. Live Spanish translation through headsets at the 11:00 AM service.

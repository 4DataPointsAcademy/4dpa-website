Why Mentorship Fails

Most mentorship programs don't work.
Here's why, and what we do instead.

A lot of our students came to us from other programs. They paid a lot, got very little help, and still didn't have a deal. We built 4 Data Points Academy around the support those programs left out.

Why mentorship programs don't work

High prices, limited support, and someone else's agenda.

They charge for information

Historically, companies charged high dollar amounts for information you could mostly find elsewhere. The price was high. The help after the sale was not.

Support is an email address

When you got stuck on a real deal, support meant emailing to schedule an appointment, or very basic customer service. Nobody was there when the deal was actually happening.

They train you to fund their projects

Many programs were really training people to invest in the company's own projects. Your money ended up building their portfolio, not yours.

The part nobody says out loud

Nobody becomes a seasoned investor in a few months.

Programs that promise it either know better or they don't. Neither one helps you. Real investors are built over years of real deals, with someone in your corner the whole way.

Odis D. James III, Founder

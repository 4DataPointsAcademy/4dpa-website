Questions we hear every week

Sole proprietor, LLC or C corp? It depends.

There's no one-size-fits-all answer to structure, an EIN or funding. The right choice depends on your answers to the first areas in this blueprint. Start there, then talk it through on a business strategy call.

How do I start a business?With a strategy session and an honest look at your strengths, weaknesses, opportunities and threats.
Do I need a business plan first?Not first. Work through the 8 areas, and the plan almost writes itself.
Should I get funding?Funding can hurt you if there's no way to make money yet. Build that first. We can help with funding when it's time.

How it works together

From first visit to repeat customer, without you lifting a finger

  1. A visitor arrives

    From your social posts, email or ads, they land on your sales page and see exactly what they get.

  2. They pay in seconds

    Stripe checkout takes the payment. The money goes to your account.

  3. The product delivers itself

    Their download link arrives by email right away, with no manual sending.

  4. Follow-up keeps working

    Buyers get a welcome sequence. People who left checkout get a reminder.

  5. Every buyer is recorded

    The CRM tags each customer, ready for your next product or offer.

You make the sale once in the system, and it repeats. No chasing payments, emailing files or tracking buyers in a spreadsheet. Each step hands off to the next automatically.

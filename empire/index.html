The problem

A website is not a business system.

Most companies can build a website. Far fewer understand the systems a business needs behind it. A website can bring someone to your door. It can't answer them at 9 PM, book the appointment, send the reminder, collect the payment or ask for the review.

A website on its own
  • Leads land in an inbox and wait
  • Missed calls stay missed
  • Booking happens by phone tag
  • Payments and invoices live somewhere else
  • Follow-up depends on your memory
A business system
  • Every lead is captured in one place
  • Missed calls get a text back automatically
  • Clients book themselves, reminders go out
  • Invoices and payments connect to the customer
  • Follow-up runs on its own

Questions

Before you start

Is the business review really free?

Yes. The intake and Odis's written recommendation are free, with no obligation. You only pay if you choose a package.

Do I need to have a business already?

No. Empire works for a business you're starting and for one that's already running. The intake tells us where you are.

I already have a website. Do I start over?

Not necessarily. The intake asks what you already have, and we recommend only what's missing.

What does the monthly fee cover?

Hosting, platform and CRM access, email and text tools, maintenance, changes to your system and the basic third-party services it runs on. Ad spend isn't included, and any usage beyond the basics is reviewed with you before it's billed.

How long does a build take?

Most core systems take about two to three weeks. Some steps need your input, like your content and logins, and add-ons can extend the timeline.

Can I cancel?

You can cancel the monthly plan anytime. You keep your domain, content and customer list. Setup fees are non-refundable because work begins as soon as you enroll.

Is there a payment plan?

No. Empire setup is paid in full.

Can I start small and upgrade later?

Yes. Start with Cornerstone and move up to Foundation or Builder anytime by paying the difference in setup.

Does Cornerstone include the AI assistant?

No. Cornerstone sends automatic replies to new leads, but the AI assistant that holds real conversations comes with Empire Builder.

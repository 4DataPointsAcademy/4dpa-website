Questions

Travel and logistics

Do I need a passport or visa?

You need a valid passport. Visa requirements depend on your citizenship, so check your government's current requirements before booking. Our team can provide supporting documents if you need them.

What meals are included?

Private Room and VIP guests get a light breakfast daily and dinner every night at the villa. Attend Only and Virtual don't include meals.

Can I come if I don't have a business yet?

Yes. The retreat is built for people with an idea, people who've started, and established owners.

What language are sessions in?

English.

What if I can't attend after I register?

In-person packages are non-refundable but transferable. Virtual Access is non-refundable. If VentureU cancels, you get a full refund; if it reschedules, you get credit toward the new dates.

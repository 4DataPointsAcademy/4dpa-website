About us

A church for people who are still figuring it out.

Lumen Valley started in a living room in 1998 with eleven people. Today we are a family of all ages, backgrounds and stories, still saving seats for whoever comes next.

Help people find their way home to God, and walk with them once they do.

What we believe

Our beliefs, in plain words

God

There is one God, Father, Son and Holy Spirit, who made us and loves us.

Jesus

Jesus lived, died and rose again so that anyone can be forgiven and made new.

The Bible

The Bible is God's word, true and trustworthy, and our guide for life.

Salvation

Salvation is a gift of grace received by faith in Jesus, not something we earn.

The church

The church is a family of believers called to love God, love people and serve the world.

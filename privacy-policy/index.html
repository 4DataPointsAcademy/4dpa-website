Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy explains how 4 Data Points Academy, Inc. ("4DPA," "we," "us") collects, uses, and protects personal information through our websites, checkout pages, app, and programs.

2. Information We Collect

  • Information you give us: name, email, phone number, mailing and billing address, business and property information, intake and application answers, and anything you send through forms or messages.
  • Payment information: payments are processed by our payment processor. We receive confirmation of payment and limited details, such as the last four digits of your card. We do not store full card numbers.
  • Automatic information: device and usage data such as IP address, browser type, pages viewed, and referring links, collected through cookies, pixels, and similar technologies.

3. How We Use It

  • To deliver the programs, products, and services you purchase, and to manage your account
  • To communicate with you by email, phone, and text message, as you have permitted
  • To process payments and prevent fraud
  • To improve our programs, websites, and app
  • To send marketing messages, only where you have agreed, and with an easy way to opt out
  • To comply with the law

4. Text Messages

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile number or text-message consent with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing purposes. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent are excluded from every category of sharing in this policy and will not be shared with any third parties. Reply STOP to any message to opt out, or HELP for help. See our Text Messaging Terms.

5. Advertising and Cookies

We use cookies and similar tools from advertising and analytics partners, including Google and Meta, to measure how our ads and pages perform and to show our ads to people who have visited our site. You can manage Google ad personalization at adssettings.google.com and opt out of interest-based advertising at optout.aboutads.info. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

6. How We Share It

We share personal information only with service providers that help us run our business (such as our CRM and website platform, payment processor, email and text-messaging providers, and video-meeting tools), when required by law, or as part of a sale or merger of our business. We require service providers to protect the information they receive. We do not sell personal information.

7. Security

We maintain reasonable security measures to protect personal information, as required by Nevada law (NRS 603A.210). No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you as the law requires.

8. Your Choices

  • Unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in any email, or reply STOP to texts.
  • Ask to see, correct, or delete your personal information by emailing info@4datapoints.com. We may need to keep some information to meet legal or accounting obligations.
  • Nevada residents: we do not sell personal information. You may still submit a request to opt out of any future sale by emailing info@4datapoints.com, as provided by NRS 603A.340.

9. Children

Our services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy and will post the updated version with a new effective date.

The story

He grew up in the room where deals get made.

Odis grew up going to real estate investment meetings with his father, Odis D. James Jr., who has been licensed in real estate since 1971. As a young child, long before he owned property, he was listening to investors talk through numbers, risk and what makes a deal worth doing.

Then he learned it from the ground floor.

After serving in the U.S. Navy until 1998 and spending a few years in telecom, he started in 2001 as a leasing consultant at an apartment community and earned his own real estate license in 2002. He worked his way up to the corporate level of the multifamily industry, overseeing 2,860 apartment units and roughly $650 million in multifamily assets for the companies he worked for, leading teams and working alongside construction and operations crews.

Along the way he ran a mortgage brokerage that funded more than $1 billion in loans, built a national notary signing agent company, and built a seven-figure company that started with $585.

He also spent three years in ministry, and it shaped how he mentors: with patience, honesty and a long view.

Why 4 Data Points Academy exists

Too many people were being taught by people who had never done it.

At one point it felt like everyone had decided they were qualified to teach real estate investing. Odis was mentoring a lot of students and needed a way to protect them, even when he wasn't in the room. That became the 4 Data Points: four questions to ask before you buy any property. What does it cost? What will it cost to fix up? What will it be worth when it's done? How much income can it generate?

You don't need his background. That's what the 4 Data Points are for.

It's also why the mentorship has no end date. Real estate doesn't stop at the end of a twelve-week program, so the support doesn't either.

In 2021 he left corporate America to build 4 Data Points Academy full time. Today it includes real estate mentorship, done-for-you business systems through Empire, personal development through AscendU, and the VentureU International Business Retreat.

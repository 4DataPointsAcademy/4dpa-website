The problem / 01

A website is not a business system.

A website can bring someone to your door. It can't answer them at 9 PM, book the appointment, send the reminder, collect the payment or ask for the review. That takes connected systems.

A website on its own

Disconnected
  • Leads land in an inbox and wait
  • Missed calls stay missed
  • Booking happens by phone tag
  • Payments and invoices live somewhere else
  • Follow-up depends on your memory

A connected business system

Empire
  • Every lead is captured in one place
  • Missed calls get a text back automatically
  • Clients book themselves, and reminders go out
  • Invoices and payments connect to the customer
  • Follow-up runs on its own

A website is useless without the systems, workflows and automations to support it. A business needs a command center. A website is just a piece of it.

Questions / 08

Before you build.

Is the business review really free?

Yes. The intake and Odis's written recommendation are free, with no obligation. You only pay if you choose a package, and you can enroll directly without it.

Do I need to have a business already?

No. Empire works for a business you're starting and for one that's already running. The intake tells us where you are.

I already have a website. Do I start over?

Not necessarily. The intake asks what you already have, and we recommend only what's missing.

What does the monthly fee cover?

Hosting, platform and CRM access, email and text tools, back-end maintenance, a limited amount of adjustments and the basic third-party services your system runs on. Ad spend isn't included, and usage beyond the basics is reviewed with you before it's billed.

When does billing start?

For Cornerstone, Foundation and Builder, the setup fee and first monthly fee are charged at enrollment. For the Digital Product System, only the $997 setup is charged at enrollment; the $97/month plan starts when your system is ready to launch.

How long does a build take?

We aim for the basic structure in about 14 days, typically within two and a half weeks. Steps that need your input, like content and logins, and add-ons can extend the timeline.

Who owns what?

You own your domain, business name, logos, content, customer data and business phone number, plus the website text and graphics we create for you. The platform account and our automations, templates and methods stay with 4 Data Points Academy and are licensed to you while your plan is active.

What happens if I cancel?

Cancel the monthly plan anytime with no minimum. Your system stays active for a 14-day transition, and you get a CSV export of your contacts, a copy of your website content and help moving your domain and phone number. Setup fees are non-refundable once work begins; the only exception is if we don't start your build within 30 days of enrollment.

Can I start small and upgrade later?

Yes. Start with Cornerstone and move up to Foundation or Builder anytime by paying the difference in setup.

Does Cornerstone include the AI assistant?

No. Cornerstone sends automatic replies to new leads, but the AI assistant that holds real conversations comes with Empire Builder.

Questions

Before you request

Is 4 Data Points Academy a lender?

No. We don't lend money or make credit decisions. We review your request and connect you with a lending partner in our network that fits your deal. We may receive compensation from those partners.

Will this hurt my credit?

Sending a request doesn't pull your credit. If a lender later needs to check credit, they'll ask for your permission through their own application.

Can you help with a home I'm going to live in?

No. We only help with investment and business-purpose financing. For a primary residence, start with a licensed mortgage lender.

What happens after I send the request?

Odis reviews it. If it's a fit, you'll get a private link to a short detailed intake: business numbers for business loans, or property and deal numbers for real estate. Then we send your file to the right partner.

Do you share my information?

Only with lending partners that may be able to fund your request, and only after you check the box allowing it. We never sell your information.

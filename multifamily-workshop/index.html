The 4DPA framework

Learn to break the deal down into four data points.

Commercial multifamily can seem complicated until the information is organized around the numbers that actually drive the decision.

Cost

Understand what it truly costs to acquire and stabilize the property.

Rehab

Evaluate physical improvements, deferred maintenance and the capital plan.

Value

Understand NOI, capitalization rates and commercial valuation.

Income

Analyze rent rolls, operating statements, collections and sustainable property income.
